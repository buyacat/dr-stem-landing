Arduino UNO 17 learning projects

What the kit is

A four-wheel-drive multifunction smart car built on Arduino with Bluetooth control. The robot avoids obstacles on its own, follows a line and tracks light sources.

Learning outcome

Students learn how to wire up sensors, program the driving logic and make boards talk to each other over a wireless link. They build algorithms for autonomous vehicle control.

Features

  • Four-wheel drive
  • Bluetooth and IR remote control
  • Obstacle avoidance, line following, light tracking
  • Block-based programming and IDE support
Specification · 7
  • Operating voltage: 5 V
  • Input voltage: 6 – 9 V
  • Maximum output current: 2 A
  • Maximum power dissipation: 25 W (at 75 °C)
  • Motor speed: 200 rpm (at 5 V)
  • Ultrasonic sensor: angle < 15°, range 2 – 400 cm
  • Wireless control: IR up to 10 m, Bluetooth up to 50 m (Android/iOS)

ESP32 15 learning projects

What the kit is

An interactive smart-house model that simulates home automation: automatic doors, windows, ventilation and lighting. Every system reacts to its surroundings or is driven remotely.

Learning outcome

Students grasp the Internet of Things and learn to automate everyday processes with gas, flame and motion sensors. They pick up the basics of designing safety and comfort systems.

Features

  • Full smart-home simulation
  • Automatic doors, lighting and ventilation control
  • Gas leak, smoke and humidity detection
  • Control from a mobile app or straight from the sensors
Specification · 6
  • Operating voltage: 5 V
  • Input voltage: 6 – 9 V or USB 5 V
  • Maximum current per pin: 20 mA (digital I/O), 50 mA (3.3 V)
  • System operating current: 100 – 500 mA
  • Interfaces: analog, digital, I2C, UART
  • Sensors: motion (PIR), gas, temperature and humidity, light

Arduino Nano 5 learning projects

What the kit is

A dedicated steering wheel with tilt sensors — an accelerometer and a gyroscope. Connect it to a robot car and the robot obeys the wheel’s movements in the air.

Learning outcome

Students find out how modern motion-recognition systems and spatial sensors work. They get hands-on practice sending telemetry between two separate devices — the wheel and the car.

Features

  • Drive the car by moving the wheel
  • Wireless data link between wheel and car
  • Accurate tilt and rotation sensing
  • Room to program extra car functions
Specification · 5
  • Operating voltage: 5 V
  • Input voltage: 6 – 9 V (platform), 3.7 – 9 V (wheel)
  • Gesture sensor: accelerometer and gyroscope
  • Wireless module: Bluetooth 2.4 GHz
  • Range: 10 – 20 metres
Arduino Nano 9 learning projects

What the kit is

A walking robot built on Arduino Nano that moves on servo-driven legs.

Learning outcome

Children meet the basics of bio-robotics and the balancing mechanisms behind walking machines. Block-based programming lets even the youngest students set up the robot’s walk and dance moves.

Features

  • Walking robot with 4 degrees of freedom (4 DOF)
  • Mimics the hops and gait of living creatures
  • Control from a graphical app on iOS and Android
  • Ideal for studying servos and biomechanics
Specification · 5
  • Operating voltage: 5 V
  • Input voltage: 7 – 12 V (or a 3.7 – 7.4 V battery)
  • Drive: 4× servos
  • Servo rotation angle: 0 – 180°
  • Control: Bluetooth or mobile app
Arduino UNO 10 learning projects

What the kit is

A smart solar tracker that uses photoresistors to turn the panel after the light source on its own. It also carries a display and a module for charging a phone from the sun.

Learning outcome

Students study renewable energy sources and automatic positioning in depth. They learn to program precise tracking systems driven by the difference between light sensor readings.

Features

  • Two-axis automatic sun tracking
  • Higher solar panel efficiency
  • Phone charging over a USB output
  • Light level data visualisation
Specification · 5
  • Operating voltage: 5 V
  • Input voltage: 5 – 9 V or USB
  • Orientation sensors: 4× photoresistors (LDR)
  • Positioning drive: 2× servos (9 g, 2 DOF)
  • Extra: USB output for charging mobile devices
